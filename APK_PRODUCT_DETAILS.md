## How It Works - Step by Step

### Step 1: Installation
1. Download the NVAPlayer TV APK file
2. Enable "Unknown Sources" in your Android device settings
3. Install the application on your device
4. Open the app to begin setup

### Step 2: License Activation
1. Launch the NVAPlayer TV application
2. Enter your unique License Key provided at purchase
3. The app will connect to the Admin Panel for verification
4. Once verified, your device will be activated automatically
5. **Note:** Activation requires internet connection for one-time verification

### Step 3: Content Setup

#### Option A: From Pendrive/USB
1. Connect your USB drive to the device
2. Grant storage permission when prompted
3. Browse and select videos from the USB drive
4. Start playing your content

#### Option B: From Internal Storage
1. Open the app and navigate to "Local Storage"
2. Browse through your device's internal storage
3. Select the video files you want to play
4. Enjoy seamless playback

#### Option C: From CMS (Content Management System)
1. Enter the CMS URL provided by your content provider
2. Login with your CMS credentials (if required)
3. Browse the available content categories
4. Select and stream content directly from the server

### Step 4: Start Watching
- Navigate through the content library
- Select your desired video or category
- Enjoy high-quality video playback
- Use playback controls (pause, forward, rewind, volume)
